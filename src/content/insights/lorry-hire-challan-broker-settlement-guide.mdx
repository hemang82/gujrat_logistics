## Why market truck settlements cause disputes

Transport brokers and FTL operators frequently hire market vehicles alongside their own fleet. Without a structured Lorry Hire Challan (LHC) linked to the party LR, tracking advances, halting charges and final balance payouts across dozens of truck owners becomes error-prone.

### Common challenges in lorry hire accounting

1. **Premature balance payouts**: Releasing final lorry hire balances before verifying a clean, signed Proof of Delivery (POD).
2. **Unrecorded route advances**: Cash or UPI advances given to drivers or suppliers get missed during final trip closing.
3. **Unclear trip margins**: Party freight (receivable) and lorry hire cost (payable) sit in separate notebooks, hiding the true brokerage margin.

## Linking every Party LR to its Lorry Hire Challan

By recording agreed hire freight, advances, TDS/deductions and POD status directly against the Lorry Hire Challan inside one connected system, brokers keep client receivables and supplier payables cleanly separated and settle balances with complete clarity.
