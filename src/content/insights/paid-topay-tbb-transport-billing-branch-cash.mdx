## The three freight payment modes in Indian road transport

Every LR / Bilty in India falls under one of three payment modes, and each requires different accounting treatment:

- **Paid**: Freight is collected at the origin booking counter and recorded in the origin branch cash book.
- **To-Pay**: Freight is collected from the consignee at the destination branch upon delivery.
- **TBB (To Be Billed)**: Freight is accumulated for corporate or regular clients and billed periodically via consolidated freight invoices.

### Where revenue leaks happen

1. **Unbilled TBB LRs**: Delivered TBB consignments get missed at month-end when billing teams rely on paper LR bundles.
2. **Missing extra charges**: Hamali, detention or door-delivery charges entered on the LR are forgotten on the final invoice.
3. **Branch cash mismatches**: Destination To-Pay collections are not matched back against origin dispatch challans.

## Keeping billing and branch cash books aligned

When LRs flow directly into branch cash books and a consolidated TBB billing queue, accountants can filter unbilled LRs by party, verify every surcharge, issue clean freight bills and track branch cash positions without manual spreadsheets.
