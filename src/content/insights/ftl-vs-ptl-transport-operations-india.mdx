## Why FTL and PTL need different dispatch workflows

Many Indian transport companies handle both Full Truckload (FTL) factory contracts and Part Truckload (PTL) parcel bookings. While both start with an LR / Bilty, the dispatch, transshipment and billing flows differ significantly.

### Full Truckload (FTL) workflow

1. **Single-party vehicle booking**: One consignor books the full truck for point-to-point delivery.
2. **Owned fleet or Lorry Hire**: Dispatchers assign an owned truck or issue a Lorry Hire Challan (LHC) for a market vehicle.
3. **Contract rate billing**: Freight is billed at a fixed trip rate or per-ton rate, often with halting or detention charges.

### Part Truckload (PTL) workflow

1. **Multi-party consolidation**: Dozens of small consignments from different parties are grouped onto one Lorry Challan.
2. **Crossing Memos & hub transfers**: Consignments moving through intermediate hubs or partner transporters require Crossing Memos for clear handover tracking.
3. **Mixed payment terms**: Every truck carries a mix of Paid, To-Pay and TBB consignments that settle in different ledgers.

## Running both on one connected platform

Instead of keeping separate registers for FTL and PTL, a unified Transport Management System lets teams book both from one screen, reuse party and item masters, and keep branch cash books and party billing aligned.
