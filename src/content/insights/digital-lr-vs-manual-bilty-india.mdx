## Why handwritten bilty pads create operational drag

In busy Indian transport hubs, most Lorry Receipts (LRs) are written under heavy evening time pressure before trucks depart. Handwritten carbon books make rate calculations, weight adjustments and destination handoffs error-prone.

### Three common friction points

1. **Duplicate re-typing into challans**: After writing LRs manually, clerks re-write every consignment onto Lorry Challans and Crossing Memos.
2. **Untracked To-Pay balances**: Destination counters must cross-check manual To-Pay cash collections back to origin books.
3. **Slow month-end TBB billing**: Collecting torn or faint carbon slips across branches delays corporate invoicing.

## Connecting LR booking to the entire dispatch lifecycle

When the booking counter enters the LR once inside a connected Transport Management System:

- **Lorry Challans** pull ready bookings automatically without re-typing weights or consignees.
- **E-Way Bills via GSP integration** attach directly to dispatch movements.
- **Branch Accounts & TBB Invoices** inherit exact freight charges cleanly.

## Practical takeaway for transport offices

Moving from paper bilty pads to a single connected LR record cuts counter time at peak dispatch hours and ensures every Paid, To-Pay and TBB booking flows accurately into branch ledgers and party bills.
