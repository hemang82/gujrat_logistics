## Why E-Way Bill friction slows evening dispatches

When a PTL or FTL truck carries multiple consignments, re-typing long E-Way Bill numbers and vehicle details on external portals right before departure creates dispatch bottlenecks and raises the risk of manual typos.

### Key E-Way Bill tasks in daily transport operations

1. **Linking E-Way Bills at LR booking**: Recording E-Way Bill numbers directly inside the consignment LR keeps dispatch paperwork together.
2. **Updating Part-B on vehicle changes**: When cargo transfers between vehicles at a crossing hub, updating Part-B with the new vehicle number keeps highway movement compliant.
3. **Generating Consolidated E-Way Bills (CEWB)**: Bundling multiple E-Way Bills loaded onto one Lorry Challan into a single CEWB simplifies verification on the road.

## Using GSP integration inside your TMS

With GST Suvidha Provider (GSP) integration built directly into your Transport Management System, dispatch teams generate E-Way Bills, update Part-B and create CEWBs from existing LR and challan records—while keeping API request logs per branch for audit visibility.
